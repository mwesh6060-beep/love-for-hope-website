Who we are

Hope begins when opportunity reaches a child.

Love for Hope is an organisation committed to giving hope to Kenyan society through access to quality education.

Many capable children face barriers such as school fees, uniforms, books and other basic learning needs. We work to close that gap by helping deserving students enrol, remain in school and prepare for the future.

  • Support for students from less fortunate backgrounds
  • Scholarship consideration based on a family's level of need
  • Verification and assessment designed to promote fairness
  • Partnerships with schools, communities and well-wishers

Our vision

A Kenya where every child, regardless of family background, has the opportunity to learn, grow and reach their full potential.

Our mission

To provide scholarships and educational support to deserving students aged 9 to 18 from less fortunate families.

Our approach

A careful, needs-based process

The organisation profile describes the following steps for students seeking support.

Registration

Student registration through a parent or guardian with the agent.

Document verification

Check legal documents, birth certificate and school enrolment proof.

Eligibility approval

Confirm the student meets the stated eligibility requirements.

Home assessment

Understand the family's circumstances and level of need.

Scholarship decision

Determine support based on the home assessment findings.

Important: The profile lists a KSh 1,250 registration/verification fee. Families should confirm all requirements and payment arrangements directly with Love for Hope using its official contact details before making any payment. This website does not collect fees or student documents.
